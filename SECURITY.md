# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email annaarivan.a@northeastern.edu with a description, reproduction steps and impact.
Please do not open public issues for vulnerabilities or test against other people's data.

- Acknowledgement: within 5 business days.
- Scope: https://arivsai.app and this repository.
- Out of scope: denial of service, social engineering, third-party services (Clerk, Google Gemini, Vercel).

This is a personal project; there is no bounty, but good-faith reports are credited on request.
